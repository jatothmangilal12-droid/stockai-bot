📱 Android App (APK) Installation

You can run this application on your Android phone in two easy ways:


Method 1: Instant 1-Tap PWA App on Phone (Zero Setup)

  1. Connect your Android phone and PC to the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Find your PC IP address (e.g. 192.168.1.X:8000).
  3. Open Chrome on your phone, navigate to http://<PC_IP>:8000.
  4. Tap Chrome menu (⋮) -> "Install app" or "Add to Home screen". It installs as a full standalone app with app icon on your phone!

Method 2: Compile Native Android APK with Capacitor

We have included a full android_app project folder with automated scripts to bundle and compile a native .apk file.
